# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee attrition, demographics, job satisfaction, education, salary, age, and years of service.

The dashboard provides an easy-to-understand view of workforce trends and helps identify factors that may influence employee attrition.

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard transforms employee data into meaningful visual insights using Power BI.

The dashboard focuses on:

* Employee Attrition
* Employee Demographics
* Age Group Analysis
* Education Field Analysis
* Job Satisfaction
* Years at Company
* Average Salary
* Average Age
* Employee Gender Distribution
* Job Role Analysis

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze employee attrition patterns.
* Identify age groups with higher attrition.
* Understand employee distribution across education fields.
* Analyze job satisfaction across different job roles.
* Examine the relationship between years at the company and attrition.
* Understand workforce demographics.
* Analyze employee distribution by gender.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Employee Attrition by Age Group

This visualization analyzes employee attrition across different age groups.

The dashboard compares:

* 18–25
* 26–35
* 36–45
* 46–55
* 55+

This helps identify the age groups experiencing the highest employee attrition.

---

### 2. Attrition by Years at Company

The area chart analyzes employee attrition based on the number of years employees have spent at the company.

This helps identify whether employees are more likely to leave during the early stages of their careers or after several years of service.

---

### 3. Employee Attrition by Education Field

The donut chart shows employee attrition across different education fields, including:

* Life Sciences
* Medical
* Marketing
* Technical Degree
* Other
* Human Resources

---

### 4. Job Satisfaction Analysis

The job satisfaction visualization compares satisfaction levels across different job roles.

It helps understand how job satisfaction varies between roles such as:

* Laboratory Technician
* Sales Executive
* Research Scientist
* Sales Representative
* Human Resources
* Manufacturing Director
* Healthcare Representative
* Manager
* Research Director

---

### 5. Gender Distribution

The dashboard provides an overview of employee distribution by gender.

* Female: 588
* Male: 882
